A house named for its wood

Someone spent years milling local trees into this place. The game room floor is Arkansas hickory with black walnut inlay. The ceiling is whitewashed maple, patterned in walnut. The walls are slabs of dogwood, catalpa, sweet gum, black locust, mimosa, persimmon, osage orange and cypress, with walnut set between every one.

Outside, it's 15 acres of Ozark woods, a deck that looks west over the hills, and a fire pit in the yard. Town is a mile away. It doesn't feel like it.

Just updated: new flooring, new furniture, and fresh felt on the pool table.

Nearby

You're a few minutes from the Buffalo National River and the Arkansas Grand Canyon overlook.

  • Arkansas Grand CanyonThe overlook on Highway 7 south of Jasper, with miles of Ozark ridges below. Best near sunset.

Good to know

How many people does Morwood House sleep?

Up to 8: three bedrooms with queen beds plus a sleeper sofa in the living room. There are two full bathrooms.

Is there a hot tub?

Yes. It's on the back deck facing west over the hills, so you get the sunset from the water.

Does it have a full kitchen?

Yes. Full size fridge, stove, oven, microwave, toaster, coffee maker, cookware, dishes and basic spices, plus a gas grill on the deck and a dining table for eight.

Where is it?

One mile south of downtown Jasper, Arkansas, just off Highway 7 (Scenic Byway 7), on 15 private acres. The Arkansas Grand Canyon overlook is a few minutes further south.

Can we bring a dog?

Ask before you book and we'll let you know what works for your dates.

Is there an age requirement?

The person booking must be 21 or older. Children are welcome. Smoking is not allowed.

Can we use it for a birthday or reunion?

Plenty of guests do. Overnight guests are capped at 8. For bigger groups, add cabins at Little Switzerland, 3.5 miles north, and call Matt before planning a gathering.

Is there EV charging?

Yes, there's an EV charger on site and free parking.
